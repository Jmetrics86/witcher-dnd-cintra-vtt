## 1. ADVENTURE BACKGROUND & LORE

A century before Geralt of Rivia walked the Path, Cintra was at the zenith of its feudal chivalry. Beneath the banner of the Rampant Golden Lion, knights joust in tourneys, elven lands are pushed toward the southern riverbanks of the Yaruga, and witchers are a familiar sight on the highways—distrusted, but essential.

In the Marnadal frontier of lower Cintra, royal timber concessions have stalled. Convoys returning from the primeval Blackwood have been annihilated: horses eviscerated, wagons splintered, and royal woodsmen strung from branches like scarecrows. 

Baron Raymond of Marnadal has posted a staggering bounty: **2,000 Cintrian Orens** for the head of the "Woodland Demon." But behind this royal notice lies an ancient betrayal: the Baron’s logging crew did not merely cut timber; they plundered the burial mound of King Elidyr, desecrated the Sacred Heart Tree, and stole the **Sun Stone of Marnadal**, an elven relic that bound the Ancient Leshen in a slumber of concord.

Now, four lone witchers find themselves under the roof of the **Lion & Lily Waystation**, each bearing the medallion of their respective school:
- **School of the Wolf:** Versatile martial tactician.
- **School of the Cat:** Lethal critical ambush fencer.
- **School of the Griffin:** Scholarly master of signs and magical wards.
- **School of the Bear:** Indomitable heavy armor juggernaut.

## 2. WITCHER MECHANICS FOR D&D 5e

### A. Vigor & The Five Signs
Each witcher has a pool of **Vigor points** (typically 4, Griffin has 6). Casting a Sign expends 1 Vigor point. Vigor fully recharges on a Short or Long Rest, or through the *Tawny Owl* potion.

1. **Aard (1 Vigor):** Action or Bonus Action. 15-foot cone. Targets must make a Strength save (DC 13, DC 15 for Griffin) or take **2d6 Force damage** and be knocked **Prone** and pushed 15 ft backwards.
2. **Igni (1 Vigor):** Action or Bonus Action. 15-foot cone. Targets take **3d6 Fire damage** (Dexterity save for half). Deals **double damage (6d6)** against the Ancient Leshen.
3. **Quen (1 Vigor):** Action or Reaction when hit. Creates a kinetic shield granting **15 Temporary HP** (25 for Griffin). Absorbs damage before actual HP is touched.
4. **Yrden (1 Vigor):** Action. Inscribes a 15-foot radius magic trap. Halves enemy movement speed, removes reaction attacks, and forces incorporeal monsters (Barghests, Specters) to become fully solid, removing their damage resistances.
5. **Axii (1 Vigor):** Action. 30-foot range. Target must make a Wisdom save or be **Stunned and Charmed** until the end of its next turn. Advantage on social roleplay checks.

### B. Toxicity & Elixirs
Each potion imbibed adds **Toxicity (15% to 30%)**. 
- Exceeding **100% Toxicity** induces severe fever, disadvantage on attack rolls, and 1d10 poison damage each turn.
- Drinking **White Honey** immediately resets Toxicity to 0% and clears all potion effects.
- Meditating during a Short Rest drops Toxicity by 30%.

### C. Twin Blades: Steel & Silver
- **Steel Swords:** Used against humanoids, bandits, soldiers, and mundane beasts.
- **Silver Swords:** Used against Relicts, Necrophages, Specters, and Cursed Ones. Deals +2d6 silver/radiant damage against monsters and bypasses all damage resistances.

## 3. THE FOUR PRE-GENERATED WITCHERS

### 1. VANE OF KAER MORHEN (School of the Wolf)
- **Role:** Balanced frontliner, parry tactician, tracker.
- **Stats:** STR 14 (+2), DEX 18 (+4), CON 16 (+3), INT 12 (+1), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 10 (+0)
- **Combat Stats:** AC 16 | HP 49 | Speed 35 ft | Vigor 4 | Passive Perception 18
- **Masterwork Silver Longsword:** +7 to hit, 1d8+4 slashing + 2d6 silver vs monsters.
- **School Perk - Fluid Riposte:** Reaction when hit by a melee attack: spend 1 Vigor or reaction to roll 1d10+4 damage reduction. If reduced to 0, make an immediate free melee counter-strike.
- **Key Potions & Gear:** Swallow (heal 2d4+3/rnd), Thunderbolt (+3 dmg), Grapeshot Bombs, Relict Oil.

### 2. SYBILLA "THE LYNX" (School of the Cat)
- **Role:** Agile assassin, dual fencer, ambush damage.
- **Stats:** STR 10 (+0), DEX 20 (+5), CON 14 (+2), INT 14 (+2), WIS 12 (+1), CHA 12 (+1)
- **Combat Stats:** AC 17 | HP 44 | Speed 40 ft | Vigor 4 | Stealth +11
- **Silver Estoc & Dual Steel Sabres:** +8 to hit, 1d8+5 silver + 2d6 sneak ambush damage.
- **School Perk - Predatory Agility & Lethal Edge:** Critical hits on a d20 roll of **19 or 20**. Bonus Action Dash/Disengage. Extra 2d6 ambush damage on targets engaged with allies.
- **Key Potions & Gear:** Blizzard (bullet-time reflex), Samum Blinding Bomb, Necrophage Oil.

### 3. ALDEN OF KAER SEREN (School of the Griffin)
- **Role:** Sign weaver, arcane scholar, noble knight-witcher.
- **Stats:** STR 12 (+1), DEX 14 (+2), CON 16 (+3), INT 18 (+4), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 14 (+2)
- **Combat Stats:** AC 17 | HP 52 | Speed 30 ft | Vigor 6 | Sign Save DC 15
- **Consecrated Silver Knightly Sword:** +6 to hit, 1d8+2 + 2d6 silver + 1d8 fire when Igni primed.
- **School Perk - Master of Signs:** Vigor pool is 6. After casting any Sign, can make a weapon strike as a Bonus Action. Quen absorbs +10 extra damage (25 Temp HP total).
- **Key Potions & Gear:** Petri's Philter (+1d8 Sign damage, disadvantage on enemy saves), Tawny Owl, Moon Dust Bomb.

### 4. BRAM OF HAERN CADUCH (School of the Bear)
- **Role:** Unstoppable tank, heavy two-handed executioner strikes.
- **Stats:** STR 18 (+4), DEX 12 (+1), CON 18 (+4), INT 10 (+0), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 8 (-1)
- **Combat Stats:** AC 18 | HP 58 | Speed 30 ft | Vigor 4 | Hit Dice: 5d12
- **Ursine Great Silver Cleaver:** +7 to hit, 2d6+4 slashing + 2d6 silver. Heavy, Two-Handed.
- **School Perk - Ursine Juggernaut:** Cannot be knocked prone or forcibly moved. When Quen is active, gains resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage. Greatsword crits knock enemies Prone.
- **Key Potions & Gear:** Northern Wind (Freeze bomb), Maribor Forest (+2 free Vigor), Relict Oil.

## 4. ACT I: THE LION & THE LILY (0:00 - 0:45)
**Location:** The Crossroads Waystation, Royal Highway of Cintra.

### Read Aloud:
> *"Autumn rain batters the heavy oak timbers of the Lion & Lily waystation like hailstones. Inside, the peat fire sputters green, casting long shadows across weary traders, Cintrian foot-soldiers, and local woodsmen nursing sour dark ale.*
> 
> *At four separate corners of the hall sit four figures who do not blend into the crowd: the unmistakable slit-pupil gaze of mutated eyes, the dull glint of twin swords strapped to their backs, and the silver medallions resting on their chests—a Wolf, a Cat, a Griffin, and a Bear.*
> 
> *The heavy cross-timber doors bang open against the wind. In strides Sir Danek, royal bailiff to Baron Raymond of Marnadal, flanked by four armored Cintrian spearmen bearing the rampant gold lion on their blue surcoats. He nails a parchment sealed with golden wax to the hearth pillar.*
> 
> *'Two thousand Cintrian orens!' Danek bellows over the crack of thunder. 'By royal decree of Baron Raymond! Two thousand pieces of good gold for the head of the demon slaughtering our royal logging convoys along the Yaruga ribbon! Do we have true men in this hall... or only cowards?'"*

### GM Flow:
1. **Introductions:** Each player describes how their Witcher reacts, sits by the fire, and touches their medallion.
2. **Contract Negotiation:** Danek tries to insult or divide them. A DC 13 Persuasion or Axii check secures a split of 500 orens each up front.
3. **Investigation:** A mute lumberjack named Kaelan sits nursing claw wounds. A DC 13 Perception check confirms fungal rot indicative of an Ancient Leshen.

## 5. ACT II: THE TRACKER'S TRAIL (0:45 - 1:45)
**Location:** The Primeval Blackwood of the Yaruga Verge.

### Read Aloud:
> *"The rain slows to a hanging, impenetrable mist as you push your horses into the Blackwood. The pine needles muffle every hoofbeat. Ahead lies the massacre. Three heavy timber wagons have been overturned and smashed like kindling. Stripped human skeletons hang from spruce branches above, tied with woven vines and crowned with crow feathers.*
> 
> *A low, unnatural chill sweeps through the fog. The hair on the back of your neck rises as your medallions vibrate furiously against your chest armor. Out from the gloom, four pairs of glowing emerald eyes ignite like corpse candles."*

### Combat: 4 Barghest Spectral Hounds (CR 2 each)
- **Tactic:** Pack Tactics (advantage on attacks when flanking). Incorporeal movement.
- **Witcher Counters:** Yrden traps strip their incorporeal resistance. Moon Dust bombs lock them into physical form. Silver blades with Specter Oil carve through them.

## 6. ACT III: SECRETS OF THE BARROW (1:45 - 2:30)
**Location:** The Forgotten Crypt of King Elidyr.

### Miniboss Encounter: Marnadal Grave Hag (CR 4)
- **Combat Mechanics:** AC 14, 68 HP. Multiattack with septic claws and a 15-foot **Tongue Lash** that pulls witchers into grave mud.
- **Targeted Strike:** Players can target the tongue (-2 to hit, 15+ damage) to sever it permanently!

### The Dark Revelation:
Inspecting the sarcophagus reveals the Truth: Baron Raymond broke the ancient covenant of Cintra. His men looted the crypt to steal the **Sun Stone of Elidyr**, cutting into the ancient sacred Heart Tree to extract primal power. The Ancient Leshen was awoken to defend the forest against human sacrilege!

## 7. ACT IV: THE HEART OF THE WOOD (2:30 - 3:30)
**Location:** The Corrupted Monolith Stone Circle.

### Read Aloud:
> *"The mist parts as you step into the sacred stone ring. Giant monoliths, older than human speech, stand like silent sentinels. In the center rises the colossal Heart Tree—its trunk split open by human axes, weeping sap as black as dried blood.*
> 
> *Antlers scrape against stone. Emerging from behind the monoliths, twelve feet tall, stands the Ancient Leshen. Its stag-skull head tilts toward you; beneath the eye sockets, twin embers of crimson hate ignite. A murder of hundreds of black ravens erupts from its shoulders with deafening caws.*
> 
> *From the treeline behind it, the earth shakes with thunderous footsteps as the Primeval Chort charges into the ring, its third eye blazing with blinding golden fury!"*

### Boss Fight: Ancient Leshen (CR 8, 135 HP) & Primeval Chort (CR 6, 112 HP)
- **Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP):** Leshen teleports via raven swarms, summons root strangles, and regenerates 15 HP/round from 3 wooden totems (AC 12, 20 HP each).
- **Phase 2 (50% HP):** The Chort charges the line! Bram (Bear) tanks the Chort with Quen bulwark while Sybilla (Cat) and Alden (Griffin) ignite the Leshen with Igni (takes double damage: 6d6 fire!).
- **Sun Stone Gambit:** Holding up the stolen Sun Stone with an action stuns the Leshen for 1 full round.

## 8. EPILOGUE: THE PRICE OF NEUTRALITY (3:30 - 4:00)

1. **Choice A: The Mercenary Professional**
   - Decapitate the Leshen, bring the stag skull trophy to Baron Raymond for 2,000 gold. The Blackwood slowly withers over decades.
2. **Choice B: The Restorer of Ancient Concord**
   - Place the Sun Stone back into the Heart Tree. The forest blesses the witchers with permanent resistance to poisons and diseases. Expose Baron Raymond's corruption.
3. **Choice C: The Witcher's Vengeance (Extortion)**
   - Brandish both the trophy and the incriminating documents. Extort 4,000 gold from the terrified Baron under threat of reporting him directly to the High King of Cintra.
